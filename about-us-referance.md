Company Profile
Simulcast Technologies Pvt. Ltd is a private limited company established in 2019 under the Company Act of 2063 BS in Nepal. The company is focused on providing a range of services including broadcasting integration, digital video broadcasting (DVB), head-end systems (DVB-C,DVB-S2, and IPTV), and over-the-top (OTT) services. In addition to these core offerings, Simulcast Technologies also provides IT and software solutions, as well as annual maintenancecontracts (AMC) and professional team work.

As a broadcaster integrator, Simulcast Technologies helps clients with the design, installation, and maintenance of broadcast systems. This includes everything from setting up the necessary hardware and software to ensuring that all systems are functioning properly. The company is also well-versed in digital video broadcasting (DVB) technologies, which enable the transmission of digital video and audio signals over various types of networks.

Simulcast Technologies is also experienced in the design and implementation of head-end systems for digital video broadcasting. These systems are used to receive, process, and transmit digital video and audio signals to multiple destinations. In addition to traditional DVB systems, the company also has expertise in IPTV head-end systems, which allow for the delivery of television content over the internet.

In addition to its broadcasting and head-end services, Simulcast Technologies also provides IT and software solutions to its clients. This includes the development and implementation of custom software solutions to meet the specific needs of each client. The company also offers annual maintenance contracts (AMC) to ensure that all systems and software are kept up-to-date and functioning properly.

Overall, Simulcast Technologies is a full-service provider of broadcast, IT, and software solutions. With a team of experienced professionals and a focus on delivering high-quality services, the company is well-equipped to meet the needs of its clients in the rapidly-evolving world of digital media.
